# Review a demo

Run `pnpm demo:produce review demos/<name>.mp4`. Read its generated report and
all contact sheets. Automatic checks cover codec, resolution, frame rate,
streaming metadata, freezes/black frames and audio when present.

Inspect words and pictures too:

- Each heading describes the screen shown; no future or fabricated outcomes.
- Menus/dialogs are visible, and captions/callouts do not obscure their targets.
- Text is readable at ordinary playback size, with sufficient reading time.
- No secrets, real customer records or misleading frozen auction clock.
- No dead setup time, dangling tooltip, unfinished gesture or abrupt ending.
- Final duration including cards/transitions is at most 300 seconds.

Record findings with timestamps in the generated report, fix the storyboard or
re-record the take, then rebuild. Keep the result, poster, chapters and review
locally. Listening is still needed if music/narration is deliberately added;
frame inspection cannot establish audio quality.
